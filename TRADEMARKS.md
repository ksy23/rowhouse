"Rowhouse" and the Rowhouse logo are trademarks of Kugamon LLC. This policy
says how you may use them. It is short on purpose.

The Apache 2.0 licence gives you the code. It does not give you the name:
section 6 of the licence grants no permission to use the licensor's trade
names or marks, except as needed to describe where the work came from.

## You may, without asking

- Say truthfully that your product uses, works with or is built on Rowhouse by Kugamon.
- Distribute unmodified Rowhouse by Kugamon under its own name.
- Write about Rowhouse by Kugamon, teach it and review it, using the name.
- Use the name in the title of a talk, article or training course about it.

## Ask first

- Using the logo anywhere.
- Using "Rowhouse" in the name of a product, service, company, domain or
  AppExchange listing.

## You may not

- Call a modified version "Rowhouse". A fork needs a different name; it may
  say it is based on Rowhouse by Kugamon.
- Suggest that Kugamon LLC endorses, certifies or supports your product or
  service when it does not.
- Use a name or logo confusingly similar to Rowhouse's.
