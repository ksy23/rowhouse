# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in Rowhouse by Kugamon, as a user, reporter,
contributor or maintainer, should find it a friendly, safe and welcoming
place, whatever their experience, gender identity and expression, sexual
orientation, disability, appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age,
religion, nationality or anything else about them.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and constructive, in issues, pull requests and reviews alike.
- Help newcomers find their way in.
- Accept criticism of your work gracefully, and give it kindly.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use Rowhouse.
- Show empathy toward others.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation or discrimination of any kind.
- Insults, trolling, and personal or political attacks.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of a discussion.
- Anything else that would be out of place in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

On this repository (issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions) and
anywhere else you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to Kugamon, either by email to
[support@kugamon.com](mailto:support@kugamon.com) or through
[Submit a Case](https://www.kugamon.com/support/submit-a-case/). Every report
is reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that break this code, and may block anyone who breaks it
repeatedly or seriously, from the repository for a time or for good.
