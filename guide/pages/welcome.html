How it fits together

Six kinds of record make one grid

A grid is described by Custom Metadata records, built on the Grid Admin tab or in Setup, which deploy between orgs like any other setting. The Rowhouse Grid component reads them, and every read and write goes through Salesforce's security as the person using the grid.

Only the Grid Configuration and its columns are needed. Format rules, filters, child tables and actions are optional. Every field of every record →

What it does

Everything a list view does, and the things it can't

{{ic:keyboard}}

Spreadsheet keys

Arrows, Tab, Enter, F2, Escape, Page Up and Down, and typing to replace a value.
{{ic:edit}}

Every common field type

Text and long text, numbers, dates with a calendar, date/times, times, checkboxes, picklists, dependent and multi-select picklists, lookups and owners.
{{ic:copy}}

Copy, paste, fill, undo

Select a range, paste up to 10,000 cells from Excel or Google Sheets, fill down, and undo up to 100 steps.
{{ic:plus}}

Add records

New adds a row at the top of the grid to fill in and save with your other changes, or opens Salesforce's New form when a record needs more.
{{ic:link}}

Fields of related records

A contact's account industry or a case's account phone, beside the record's own fields and edited the same way.
{{ic:palette}}

Conditional formatting

Fills, colored text, bold, icons and data bars, on a value, a range, two fields or a formula, with a Display Key that says what each one means.
{{ic:formula}}

Formula columns

Values the grid works out from other fields, in the syntax of a formula field: days on file, a weighted amount.
{{ic:search}}

Filters

The admin's, which stay, and each user's own from a column's menu. Counts, totals and exports follow them.
{{ic:layers}}

Grouping and subtotals

Up to three levels, dates by day, week, month, quarter, year or fiscal period, with sums and averages over every matching record.
{{ic:tree}}

Related records under rows

Open a row to see its contacts, cases or deals in tabs. Each tab is a full grid of its own.
{{ic:page}}

On a record page

On an account's page, a grid of that account's contacts or deals: a related list you can edit in place.
{{ic:eye}}

Your own layout

Hide, move and resize columns, sort and group: the grid opens the same way next time, on any computer.
{{ic:bolt}}

Actions

Run a screen flow, an autolaunched flow, invocable Apex or a quick action on one row or on many.
{{ic:upload}}

Import and export

Bring in a CSV or Excel file with a preview first; export what you see to CSV.
{{ic:check}}

Change many at once

Select rows, then update one field on all of them, delete them, or run an action.
{{ic:history}}

Audit trail

Optionally log every change: who, when, which field, the value before and after.
{{ic:shield}}

Salesforce security, always

Users see and change only what their profile, permission sets and sharing allow.
{{ic:phone}}

Phones and narrow spaces

Below 480 pixels wide the grid becomes a list of record cards, tap to edit.
{{ic:gauge}}

Big lists

The first 10,000 records open at once, and more load as you scroll, up to 100,000. Counts, subtotals and filters cover every matching record.
{{ic:sort}}

Row numbers

Rows are numbered down the left edge, as in a list view, and numbered again after each sort, search or filter.
{{ic:lock}}

Encrypted fields

Fields encrypted with Shield Platform Encryption show and edit as usual; the grid leaves out the sorting, filtering and grouping Salesforce cannot do on them.
The life of an edit

Nothing is saved until you say so

Every change waits as an unsaved edit, shaded yellow, until Save. Then it is checked twice, saved in batches, and read back so formulas and automation show their real results.

Where to find this guide

This is the Grid Guide tab. Everyone with the Rowhouse Grid User permission set can open it: in the Rowhouse app, which opens on it (click the App Launcher, type Rowhouse, and click Rowhouse under Apps), from the App Launcher's search (Grid Guide, under Items), or from the ? button on any grid, which opens the User guide in a new browser tab. People who build grids also have the Grid Admin and Grid Edit Logs tabs in the app. On the Grid Admin tab, the Grid Guide button opens Get started, and while a grid is open the guide's part about the open tab sits beside it, with Open ↗ for the whole of it. The pages for admins (Get started, Configure and Features) show only to the Rowhouse Grid Admin permission set. Once you scroll past the guide's sections at the top, a round arrow, Back to top, sits at the bottom right of the page; click it to go back up to them.
