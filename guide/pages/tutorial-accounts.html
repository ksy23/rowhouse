Key accounts, with their people and deals

Hannah looks after a dozen accounts. Before each quarterly review she checks every account's contacts and open deals. Today that means opening each account and scrolling its related lists. Here, every account row opens into tabs of its contacts and opportunities.

The goal

What you will build

Three grids: one of accounts, and two that appear as tabs under each account row. The two child grids are ordinary Grid Configurations; a Grid Child Table record links each one to the account grid.

Build it · admin

1. Two child grids

Build the grids that go in the tabs first, so they can be chosen when you link them. They never need a page of their own.

3. Link them as tabs

  1. Open Grid Child Table records. In Custom Metadata Types, beside Grid Child Table, click Manage Records, then New.
  2. Create these two:
    Label · NameGrid ConfigurationChild ConfigurationRelationship FieldTab LabelSort Order
    Key Accounts Contacts
    Key_Accounts_Contacts    		Key AccountsKey Account ContactsAccountIdContacts10

Relationship Field is the field on the child record that points to the account: AccountId on both Contact and Opportunity. Rowhouse uses it to show each account's own records, and to fill it in on records imported into the tab.
Build it · admin

4. The page

  1. Create an App page in Lightning App Builder, as in the other tutorials: New › App Page › Label Key Accounts › One Region.
  2. Add the grid. Drag Rowhouse Grid onto the page and enter Key_Accounts in Grid Configuration. Only the account grid is placed; its tabs come with it.
  3. Save and activate, adding the page to the Sales app.
Use it · Hannah

5. Open an account

  1. Open the grid. In the Sales app, click Key Accounts.
    A column of toggles beside the account names. Accounts with related records show a count beside their toggle; accounts with none have no toggle.
  2. Open one. Click the toggle beside an account with contacts and opportunities (we use Edge Communications).
    A panel opens under the row, with a blue bar down its left edge and two tabs: Contacts (2) and Opportunities (4). It opens on the first tab with records.
  3. Look at the deals. Click the Opportunities tab. The deals have a Total row with the sum of their amounts.
Use it · Hannah

6. Edit in a tab

  1. Fix a title. Back on the Contacts tab, click a contact's Title once and type a new one (we use SVP, Global Procurement for Rose Gonzalez); what you type replaces the old title. Press Enter.
    The tab's own Save bar appears inside the panel. Each tab saves on its own.
  2. Save it. Click that Save bar's Save.
  3. Close the account. Click the account's toggle again.
Use it · Hannah

7. The same from the keyboard

  1. Click any cell of the account grid, then use ↑ ↓ to reach an account with a toggle, and ← until you reach the toggle itself, at the left of the row. Press Enter to open it.
  2. Press ↓ to move onto the panel, then Enter to go into the tab's grid. Move and edit there as in any grid.
  3. Press Esc to come back out to the panel, and ↑ to return to the account's row.
