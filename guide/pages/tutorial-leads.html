The morning lead queue

Jenna starts each day with the leads that came in overnight. She rates them, calls the hot ones first, and passes the rest to the team. In a list view, each of those is a record opened and saved. Here, one grid of open leads marks the hot ones nobody has called yet in red, logs a call from the row, and hands leads over in one go.

The goal

What you will build

One grid of leads, built on the Grid Admin tab. A filter leaves out closed leads. A formula rule paints a lead's row red when it is rated Hot and still not contacted; an arrow marks every Hot rating; Cold ones are gray. Log a call is in each row's menu.

Skip the build

To go straight to using the grid, click the button below, then Build in the dialog: it builds Lead Queue as steps 1 to 3 leave it, in a few seconds, while a spinner says which grid it is on. Then go on to step 4, which puts it on a page. The build steps are still the way to learn how a grid is made.

Build it · admin

1. The grid

You need the Rowhouse Grid Admin permission set, and Customize Application to save (see Give people access).

  1. Open the Grid Admin tab. Click the App Launcher, type Rowhouse, click the Rowhouse app, then the Grid Admin tab, and click New grid.
  2. Fill in the Grid tab:
    FieldValue
    Grid titleLead Queue
    API nameLead_Queue fills in by itself
    ObjectLead (Lead)
    Sort byLast Name (LastName)
  3. Add the columns. Click Columns (1). Full Name (Name) is already there. For each row below, click Add column, choose the field, and select Edit:
    FieldEdit
    Company (Company)✓
    Title (Title)✓
    Status (Status)✓
    Rating (Rating)✓
    Lead Source (LeadSource)✓
    Owner ID (OwnerId)✓
    The Owner ID column's Header box suggests Owner, in gray.

2. A filter and three display rules

  1. Leave out closed leads. Click Filters (0), then Add filter:
    LabelOpen leads
    FieldStatus (Status)
    ConditionDoes not contain
    ValueClosed
    Both closed statuses, Closed - Converted and Closed - Not Converted, contain the word, so neither shows.
  2. Mark the hot leads nobody has called. Click Display rules (0), then Add display rule, and fill in the row:
    MeaningHot, not contacted yet
    ConditionFormula is true choose it first
    Fieldleave it empty: it says The cell it colors, and a rule on the whole row needs none
    Value: the box that says Such as Amount__c > Budget__c * 1.1AND(ISPICKVAL(Rating, "Hot"), ISPICKVAL(Status, "Open - Not Contacted"))
    LookRed fill
    Applies toThe whole row
    A formula rule tests the saved record on the server, with Salesforce's own formula language: here, two fields at once.
  3. Point at every hot lead. Click Add display rule:
    MeaningHot
    FieldRating (Rating)
    ConditionEquals
    ValueHot
    LookUp arrow
  4. Quiet the cold ones. Click Add display rule once more: Meaning Cold, Field Rating (Rating), Condition Equals, Value Cold, Look Gray text.
Build it · admin

3. Log a call

Log a Call is a global quick action that every org has. From a lead's row it opens Salesforce's Log a Call window, and the call it saves goes on the lead's activity timeline, marked completed.

  1. Add the action. Click Actions (0), then Add action, and fill in the row:
    LabelLog a call what the menu says
    TypeQuick action
    RunsLogACall
    Offered inRow menu
  2. Save. Click Save. After a few seconds the editor reads the grid back: Columns (7), Display rules (3), Filters (1), Actions (1).
Build it · admin

4. The page

  1. Create an App page. In Setup, type Lightning App Builder in the Quick Find box and click Lightning App Builder. Click New, choose App Page, click Next, enter the Label Lead Queue, click Next, choose One Region, and click Done.
  2. Add the grid. Drag Rowhouse Grid from the Custom - Managed components onto the page, and enter Lead_Queue in Grid Configuration.
  3. Save and activate. Click Save, then Activate. On the Lightning Experience tab, choose Sales, click Add page to app, then Save.

5. Open the queue

  1. Open it. In the Sales app, click Lead Queue.
    The open leads by last name, 15 in a new Developer Edition org, and a locked chip Status does not contain Closed. Bertha Boxer's Rating, Hot, has an arrow before it. No row is red: Bertha has been contacted.

6. Rate a lead

Phyllis Cotton, the CFO of Abbott Insurance, asked for pricing on the website last night.

  1. Rate her Hot. Double-click Phyllis Cotton's Rating cell, choose Hot and press Enter.
    The cell is yellow, an unsaved change, and the Save bar says "1 unsaved change".
  2. Save. Click Save in the bar at the bottom.
    "Saved 1 record." An arrow comes before Hot, and Phyllis Cotton's row turns red, with a red bar at its left edge: Hot, and still Open - Not Contacted. A formula rule tests the saved record, so the red comes with Save.
Use it · Jenna

7. Call it in

  1. Log the call. After calling Phyllis, click the ▾ at the right end of her row and choose Log a call.
    Salesforce's Log a Call window opens over the grid, with Phyllis Cotton filled in.
  2. Save it. Type what you talked about in Comments if you like, and click Save.
    The window closes and you are back at the queue. The call is on her activity timeline, marked completed.
  3. Mark her contacted. Double-click her Status cell, choose Working - Contacted, press Enter, and click Save in the bar at the bottom.
    The red goes; the arrow stays. She is still hot, but no longer waiting.
Use it · Jenna

8. Hand leads over

Two leads in health care and manufacturing go to a colleague who covers those industries.

  1. Select them. Select the checkbox at the start of Mike Braund's row and of Jeff Glimpse's.
    A bar floats over the top of the grid: "2 selected", with Update field and Clear.
  2. Give them a new owner. Click Update field. In Field, choose Owner; in New Owner, type part of a colleague's name and choose them; then click Apply.
    The dialog is headed Change 2 selected rows and names the two leads. Once you click Apply, both Owner cells show your colleague's name, yellow as unsaved changes, and the Save bar says "2 unsaved changes".
  3. Save. Click Save, then Clear in the selection bar.
    The two leads are your colleague's. They stay in the queue, which shows every open lead you can see; to see only your own, filter the Owner column by Is me.
