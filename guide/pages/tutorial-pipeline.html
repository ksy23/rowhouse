Sales operations · about 25 minutes

The weekly pipeline review

Every Monday, Abigail walks the sales managers through the open pipeline. Until now she has exported opportunities to a spreadsheet, fixed them there, and asked each rep to retype the changes. This grid lets her fix them where they live.

What you will build

You need

The Rowhouse Grid User permission set, permission to customize the application, and some opportunities. Our example org has 31; the record names below are ours, so pick your own where a step names one.

2. Eight columns

3. Three display rules

  1. Open Grid Format Rule records. In Custom Metadata Types, beside Grid Format Rule, click Manage Records, then New.
  2. Create these three. In each, type the Label, then replace the Grid Format Rule Name that fills in with the name in the table. Type Pipeline Review in Grid Configuration and leave Active selected:
    Label · NameField API NameOperatorValueStyleApply ToSort Order
    Overdue
    Pipeline_Overdue    		CloseDateLess thanTODAYRed textCell10
    Big deal
    Pipeline_Big_Deal    		AmountGreater than or equal to250000Green fillCell20

    Pipeline_Negotiating    		StageNameEqualsNegotiation/ReviewPurple fillRow30
The Label is what the grid's Display Key shows, so write it for users. The Name only has to be unique across all grids. Write numbers without a currency symbol or commas: 250000, not $250,000. TODAY is today in each user's time zone. A Row rule's fill tints the whole row lightly and draws a bar at its left edge; the tint is lighter than a cell's fill, so it never hides a cell's own color.
Build it · admin

4. The page

  1. Create an App page. In Setup, type Lightning App Builder in the Quick Find box and click Lightning App Builder. Click New, choose App Page, click Next, enter the Label Pipeline Review, click Next, choose One Region, and click Done.
  2. Add the grid. Drag Rowhouse Grid from the Custom components onto the page, and enter Pipeline_Review in Grid Configuration.
  3. Save and activate. Click Save, then Activate. On the Lightning Experience tab, choose Sales, click Add page to app, then Save.
Use it · Abigail

5. Read the pipeline

  1. Open the grid. Open the Sales app and click Pipeline Review in the navigation bar (or under More).
    "Grouped by Stage" above the grid, a row for each stage with its count, the sum of its amounts, the average probability and the earliest close date, and a Total row at the bottom.
  2. Put the closed deals away. Groups follow the order of the Stage picklist, as they do in a Salesforce report: Stage: Prospecting first and Stage: Closed Won last. Scroll down the grid to Stage: Closed Won and click the arrow on its row. Its deals fold away; the Total row still counts them.
  3. Find the late ones. Red dates in the open stages are overdue. Click Display Key above the grid to see what each color means, then click it again to close it.
Closed deals have past dates too

A rule tests one field, so closed deals with past close dates are red as well. That is why the grid is grouped by stage: closed deals sit in their own groups, which Abigail keeps folded.

Use it · Abigail

6. Fix deals as the managers call them out

  1. Change an amount. Click the Amount of an open deal (we use University of AZ Installations, $100,000), type 250000 and press Enter.
    The cell turns yellow and shows $250,000. A Save bar appears at the bottom of the grid: "1 unsaved change".
  2. Move a deal to another stage. Click the Stage of another open deal (we use Edge Emergency Generator), press Enter to edit it, choose Negotiation/Review in the list (or type its first letters), and press Enter.
    The deal turns purple at once (a light tint and a bar at its left edge), but stays in its group until you save. The Save bar now says "2 unsaved changes".
  3. Give three deals the same next step. Scroll the grid sideways to Next Step (the Opportunity column stays put). Click the Next Step of the first deal in a stage with three or more deals (in our org, Id. Decision Makers), type Send revised quote and press Enter. Press Shift+↓ twice to stretch the selection over the two deals below, then press Ctrl+D (Cmd+D on a Mac) to fill down.
    All three Next Step cells say "Send revised quote", and the Save bar counts the new changes.
  4. Change your mind, then change it back. Press Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z): the fill down is undone in one step. Press Ctrl+Shift+Z (Cmd+Shift+Z) to redo it. The Save bar's Undo and Redo buttons do the same.
  5. Push out an overdue date. Double-click a red Close Date in an open stage. A calendar opens under the cell (above it, if the cell is near the bottom of the grid). Click the right arrow beside the month name to go to next month, and click any day.
    The date is no longer red: it shows the day you picked, in yellow, as an unsaved change.
Use it · Abigail

7. Save and check

  1. Save. Click Save on the Save bar.
    The yellow goes. University of AZ Installations' $250,000 turns green (Big deal). Edge Emergency Generator moves to the Negotiation/Review group tinted purple with a purple bar at its left edge, and the group sums change to match. The new date is no longer red.
  2. Check a record. Click a deal's name: the opportunity opens, with the values you saved. Press your browser's Back button to return to the grid. (Cmd-click or Ctrl-click opens it in a new browser tab instead.)
