Contacts on an account page

Sam keeps every account's contact list tidy: titles change, people move, numbers go stale. The Contacts related list shows names but edits one record at a time. Here, the account page gets a grid of that account's people, editable in place, with the senior ones marked.

The goal

What you will build

One grid of contacts, built on the Grid Admin tab. Its record page field is the contact's Account, so on an account's page it shows only that account's contacts. A formula column shows how long each contact has been on file, an icon marks vice-presidents, and a filter leaves out contacts without an email.

Build it · admin

1. The grid

You need the Rowhouse Grid Admin permission set, and Customize Application to save (see Give people access).

  1. Open the Grid Admin tab. Click the App Launcher, type Rowhouse, and click the Rowhouse app. It opens on the Grid Guide; click the Grid Admin tab, then New grid.
  2. Fill in the Grid tab:
    FieldValue
    Grid TitleAccount People
    API nameAccount_People fills in by itself
    ObjectContact (Contact)
    Sort byLast Name (LastName)
    On a record page, show the records whoseAccount ID (AccountId), on Account pages
    The last one is what ties the grid to the page it sits on: on an account's page it shows only the contacts whose Account is that account. The list offers each lookup of Contact to one object, with the pages it works on.
  3. Add the columns. Click Columns (1). Full Name (Name) is already there. For each row below, click Add column, choose the field, and tick Edit where the table says so:
    FieldEdit
    Title (Title)✓
    Email (Email)✓
    Business Phone (Phone)✓
    Account ID (AccountId)—
    The Account column's Header box suggests Account, in gray: a lookup is headed by what it points to, so there is no need to type one.
  4. Keep the name on screen. On the Full Name (Name) row, tick Always shown.
    Users can hide and move the grid's columns for themselves (step 6). This one they can move, but not hide.

2. A formula, a rule and a filter

  1. Add a formula column. Click Add formula column. In the new row:
    Field column: the box that says Such as Amount__c * 0.2TODAY() - DATEVALUE(CreatedDate)
    The list under it (what the formula returns)Number already chosen
    The box beside the list, 2 places (decimal places)0
    Header column, the box that says RequiredDays on file
    Until the formula and the header are filled in, the editor lists them under Fix these before saving. A formula with a mistake shows Salesforce's own message there. The line under the buttons says 1 of 10 formulas used.
  2. Mark the vice-presidents. Click Display rules (0), then Add display rule, and fill in the row:
    MeaningExecutive
    FieldTitle (Title)
    ConditionContains
    ValueVP
    LookInfo icon
    Applies toThe cell already chosen
  3. Leave out contacts without an email. Click Filters (0), then Add filter:
    NameHas email
    FieldEmail (Email)
    ConditionIs not blank
  4. Save. Click Save. After a few seconds the editor reads the grid back: Columns (6), Display rules (1), Filters (1).
Build it · admin

3. The account page

  1. Open an account, for example United Oil & Gas Corp.
  2. Edit its page. Click the gear at the top right, then Edit Page. Lightning App Builder opens with the account's page.
  3. Add the grid. In the components list on the left, type Rowhouse in the search box, and drag Rowhouse Grid (under Custom) onto the page, above the Related and Details tabs. On the right, enter Account_People in Grid Configuration.
    The grid shows this account's contacts straight away, in the editor. There is nothing to set for the record: Lightning gives the grid the record of the page it is on.
  4. Save and activate. Click Save. If Salesforce asks, click Activate; otherwise click Activation…. Choose Assign as Org Default, then Desktop and phone, click Next, then Save. Click the back arrow at the top left to return to the account.

4. Open an account

  1. Open United Oil & Gas Corp. The grid Account People is on its page.
Five contacts: Lauren Boyle, Molly Fitzgerald, Avi Green, Stella Pavlova and Arthur Song. Under the title: 5 items • Filtered • Sorted by LastName, and a locked chip Email is not blank: the admin's filter. An i icon marks the three titles with VP in them (SVP, Technology; VP, Sales; SVP, Production). The Account column is headed Account. Hover over a Days on file cell and it shows a lock: the grid works it out.

Title, Email and Business Phone are edited in place, as in any grid: double-click, type, Enter, then Save in the bar at the bottom.

Use it · Sam

5. Narrow it down

  1. Filter by title. Click the ▾ on the Title header, then Filter…. Contains is chosen; type VP in Value and press Enter.
    Three contacts. A chip Title contains "VP" joins the admin's, with an ✕, and the Title header shows a filter icon.
  2. Take it off. Click Clear filters. The five contacts are back; the admin's chip stays.
Use it · Sam

6. Make it yours

  1. Sort by title. Click the Title header.
  2. Hide a column. Open the ▾ menu on the Business Phone header and choose Hide column.
    The column goes. The ▾ menu on Full Name has no Hide column: the admin keeps it on screen.
  3. Move one. Drag the Email header by its name onto the left half of the Title header, and let go.
    A blue bar shows on Title's left edge while you drag; Email then sits before Title.
  4. Reload the page (⌘+R, or Ctrl+R).
    The grid opens as Sam left it: sorted by Title, Email before Title, no Business Phone. A ↶ button, Reset layout, is beside Refresh. The filter from step 5 is gone: filters are for the visit.
  5. Bring a column back. Click Choose columns, the button with three bars beside Refresh. Click Business Phone under Available, click ▸ to move it to Visible, and click Save.
    Business Phone is back, last. In the dialog, Full Name had a lock beside it: it cannot leave Visible.
  6. Put it back. Click Reset layout. The grid is sorted by last name again, its columns are as the admin set them, and the button goes.
Use it · Sam

7. Add a contact

United Oil & Gas has a new buyer, and Sam adds her from the account's page.

  1. Click New above the grid.
    Salesforce's New Contact form opens over the page, headed New Contact, with When it is saved, it is added to the grid. under it. Account Name already says United Oil & Gas Corp.: the grid shows this account's contacts, so it fills that in.
    A new contact needs a last name, and this grid has no column to type one in: Full Name is made from First Name and Last Name. When a row cannot hold what a new record needs, New opens Salesforce's own form instead.
  2. Fill it in:
    FieldValue
    First NameNora
    Last NamePickering
    TitlePurchasing Manager
    Emailnora.pickering@example.com the grid's filter leaves out contacts without one
  3. Save. Click Save.
    The form closes and Nora Pickering is at the top of the grid, where you can see her: 6 items. Click Refresh and she takes her place by last name, between Stella Pavlova and Arthur Song.
